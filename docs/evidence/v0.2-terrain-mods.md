## Independent original capture

The standalone original-only module captured five signed seeds across the
required 5×5 square centered at `(32,32)` and its mirrored square centered at
`(-32,-32)`, for 250 Overworld `NOISE` snapshots:

```powershell
$env:JAVA_HOME = 'C:\Program Files\Eclipse Adoptium\jdk-21.0.12.101-hotspot'
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\scripts\capture-original-corpus.ps1 `
  -Endpoint NOISE -Dimension minecraft:overworld -ContextId terralith-overworld `
  -CoreSquareSideChunks 5 `
  -ModDirectory .\build\terrain-mods\terralith-2.6.2-lithostitched-1.8.0b6-20260913 `
  -CaptureRoot .\build\oracle-captures\v02-terralith-250-20260913 `
  -RunRoot .\build\oracle-runs\v02-terralith-250-20260913
```

The capture completed in five clean 50-case server runs. The log is
`build/terralith-oracle-250-20260913.log`; the expected corpus contains 250
`.snap` files.
