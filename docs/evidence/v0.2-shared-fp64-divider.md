The new `SharedFp64DivisionStageEmitter` owns graph-independent stages. It
retains exactly the original 108 restoring-division steps, four per dispatch,
the 16-word carrier, special classifications, subnormal normalization, sticky
bit and RNE packing. Init still reads the two GPU predecessor operands from
distinct slots 4 and 6 of an eight-word row. No CPU density/result is seeded.

`IntegerIeeeEmitter.helperSource` selects exact helper declarations from that
compiler's own source once, preserves declaration order and arithmetic text,
and fails closed on unknown roots/dependencies. Divider emission no longer
repeatedly copies or analyzes the captured graph. The small chunk modules no
longer inherit unrelated blend prototypes, captured globals or blank-line
scaffolding. This is a structural cost reduction, not a measured TPS uplift.
The historically failing 29-stage resident chain remains disabled by default.

It is replaced by an owned LRU bounded at both 2,048 entries and 67,108,864
binary bytes. Returned/inserted modules are copied; invalid input cannot
change storage. Oversized modules execute uncached, and replacement, byte/count
eviction and shutdown accounting are tested. Native pipeline limits, fence,
quarantine and buffer budgets remain unchanged. `spirvCacheTelemetry` is
recorded separately from native storage in both candidate receipt types.
